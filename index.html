How ratings work. Elo across every completed Ultimate singles set. Everyone starts at 1500; K-factor 32; each set counts once (best-of-N is a single Elo event, not per-game). Sets are processed in strict chronological order. Byes, doubles, and DQs are excluded. Seasons are calendar quarters — each resets every player to 1500 and is ranked independently.
